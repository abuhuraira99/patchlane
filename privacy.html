Privacy

Patchlane is built so that what you paste never leaves your device.

What happens to your input

Every check runs as JavaScript inside your browser tab. The specs, responses, tokens and tool lists you paste are read in memory and are not sent to any server, ours or anyone else's. The site is a set of static files: there is no backend to receive them.

What is stored on your device

  • Your choice of framework for fix code, in this browser's local storage.
  • Your inputs, only if you tick "Remember my inputs on this device". Untick it and they are deleted.

What the site loads

Fonts, scripts and styles are served from this site. There are no analytics, tracking pixels, advertising scripts or third-party embeds. The hosting provider, Cloudflare, keeps standard request logs (IP address, page, time) for security and operations; those logs never contain what you paste.

The CLI and the GitHub Action

Both run on your own machine or runner and make no network calls. Reports are written where you tell them to be.
