Terms of use

Short, because the product is simple: it reads what you give it and tells you what it found.

Licence

The Patchlane source code, CLI and GitHub Action are released under the MIT licence. You may use, copy, change and redistribute them under its terms.

What the findings are

Patchlane performs static review of documents you supply: an API specification, a captured response, a token, a tool list. It does not test, scan or send traffic to any system. Its findings are review notes to confirm on the running service. They are not a certification, an audit opinion or a guarantee that an API is secure.

Your responsibility

Use it on APIs and artefacts you own or are allowed to review. Test fixes before you ship them; the fix code shown is a starting point written for common setups.
